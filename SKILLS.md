# Cybersecurity Skills Matrix

This matrix tracks technical skills developed through formal education, industry certification study, hands-on training and independent projects.

Skill levels are intentionally conservative. Completing a learning exercise does not automatically mean professional proficiency.

## Legend

* 🟢 **Practised** — Used practically in a lab or project
* 🟡 **Developing** — Introduced and actively building capability
* 🔵 **Current Focus** — Currently studying and practising
* ⚪ **Future** — Planned development
