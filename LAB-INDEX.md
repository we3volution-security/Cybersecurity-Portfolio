# Evidence Standard

For each significant hands-on lab, I aim to document:

### 1. Environment

What system, machine or environment was used?

### 2. Objective

What was the technical or security objective?

### 3. Practical Work

What did I actually do?

### 4. Skills

What technical capability did the exercise demonstrate?

### 5. Security Relevance

Why does this matter in a real IT or security environment?

### 6. Evidence

What screenshots or other artefacts demonstrate the work?

### 7. Reflection

What did I understand better after completing the exercise?

---

# Evidence Policy

This portfolio intentionally does **not** publish:

* Training flags
* Answers
* Passwords
* Private keys
* Personal information
* Sensitive target information
* Complete training walkthroughs

The objective is to demonstrate technical understanding and practical ability rather than reproduce training material.
