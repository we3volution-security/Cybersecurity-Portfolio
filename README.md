# Cybersecurity Portfolio

## Practical IT & Cybersecurity Development

I'm building a practical foundation in **IT, cybersecurity and offensive security**, with a long-term interest in penetration testing and red-team operations.

This repository documents my progression through hands-on labs, technical training, certifications and independent security projects.

My approach is deliberately practical: I want to understand how systems work, interact with them directly, identify weaknesses and understand how those weaknesses can be addressed.

# Independent Cybersecurity Education Project

Alongside my formal cybersecurity training, I am developing an independent public-facing technology education platform.

The project focuses on Web3, blockchain and cryptocurrency literacy, and evolved into a deliberately **jargon-free, hype-free digital and civic literacy resource**, with an increasing focus on cybersecurity, online safety and scam awareness.

The goal is not simply to tell people what technologies are or what risks exist, but to help people **understand, recognise and safely interact with technology themselves**.

Current and planned educational areas include:

* Web3 and blockchain fundamentals
* Wallets and digital assets
* Smart contracts
* Safe interaction with Web3 applications
* Scam recognition
* Phishing awareness
* "Do Your Own Research" (DYOR)
* Common cryptocurrency and Web3 scams
* Online safety
* Security awareness
* Digital literacy
* Cybersecurity fundamentals

Where resources allow, I aim to make the learning as practical and interactive as possible rather than relying solely on written explanations.

## Long-Term Vision

My longer-term goal is to develop an **interactive, AI-assisted Web3 learning environment** where people can learn by safely experimenting inside a sandbox rather than interacting with real assets or potentially dangerous live systems.

The concept is to allow learners to practise things such as:

```text
Understand Web3
      ↓
Create / understand a wallet
      ↓
Understand transactions
      ↓
Interact with simulated applications
      ↓
Understand smart contracts
      ↓
Recognise suspicious behaviour
      ↓
Identify scams and phishing
      ↓
Develop safer digital habits
```

The emphasis is on **clarity, safety and practical understanding**.

This project is also part of my own cybersecurity development. Researching real-world scams, social engineering, malicious behaviour and safe interaction with online systems helps me identify areas where deeper cybersecurity knowledge is required.

As my cybersecurity training progresses, I intend to incorporate what I learn into the public education side of the project where appropriate.

The aim is therefore twofold:

> **Develop my own technical and cybersecurity capability while making that knowledge useful and understandable to other people.**
