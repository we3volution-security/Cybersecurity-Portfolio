# Long-Term Education Vision

The longer-term vision is to develop an interactive, AI-assisted Web3 and cybersecurity learning environment where people can safely experiment in a sandbox rather than being exposed to real financial or security risks.

The intended learning model is:

```text
Understand the technology
        ↓
Learn the risks
        ↓
Practise safely
        ↓
Recognise suspicious behaviour
        ↓
Experiment in a sandbox
        ↓
Develop safer habits
        ↓
Apply knowledge independently
```

This is both an independent project and a learning opportunity for me.

Researching scams, social engineering, malicious behaviour and safe technology use helps identify areas where deeper cybersecurity knowledge is required.

This creates a continuous feedback loop:

**Learn → understand → practise → explain → identify gaps → learn further**

# Learning Philosophy

I don't want to simply collect completed rooms, courses or certifications.

For significant practical work, I aim to answer:

> What did I interact with?

> What did I actually do?

> Why did it work?

> What security principle was involved?

> How could the weakness be prevented or detected?

> Could I reproduce the technique independently?

The objective is **practical capability supported by formal knowledge**, rather than certificate accumulation alone.
